# Series where the city or town is a character

## Recommendation

For the overlap of *Twin Peaks*, *Dark*, *Ozark*, and *Banshee*, start with
*Widow’s Bay*, *Carnivàle*, *The Kettering Incident*, and *Wayward Pines* season
1. They make the setting feel active: a cursed island, a travelling world
with its own mythology, an isolated town shaped by disappearance and ecology,
and an impossible town governed by hidden rules.

If you want stronger critical consensus, choose *Sharp Objects* or
*Yellowjackets*. If you want the most metaphysical leap, choose *The OA*. For
older, stranger occult television, choose *The Kingdom* (*Riget*).

## Shortlist and where to begin

1. *Widow’s Bay* — closest new “community with a curse” premise; Rotten
   Tomatoes 98% critics / 93% audience; Apple TV availability snapshot.
2. *Carnivàle* — the world itself feels mythic and conspiratorial; IMDb 8.4,
   JustWatch 77%, Metacritic 63; HBO Max US snapshot; unfinished after two
   seasons.
3. *The Kettering Incident* — the best remote-town/forest mystery lead; IMDb
   6.6, Rotten Tomatoes 67%, JustWatch 53%; availability is inconsistent
   across JustWatch crawls.
4. *Wayward Pines* season 1 — strongest “town as trap” hook; IMDb 7.3, Rotten
   Tomatoes 60%, JustWatch 83%; Hulu US snapshot.
5. *Sharp Objects* — grounded, poisonous small-town noir; IMDb 8.0, Rotten
   Tomatoes 92%, JustWatch 92%; HBO Max US snapshot.
6. *Yellowjackets* — wilderness becomes a psychological and supernatural
   character; IMDb 7.7, Rotten Tomatoes 92%, JustWatch 87%, Metacritic 74;
   Netflix/Paramount+ US snapshot.
7. *The OA* — most cosmic and enigmatic; IMDb 7.8, Rotten Tomatoes 84%,
   JustWatch 86%, Metacritic 63; Netflix snapshot.
8. *The Kingdom* (*Riget*) — hospital-as-haunted-world; IMDb 8.2, JustWatch
   76%, Metacritic 78; MUBI US snapshot.
