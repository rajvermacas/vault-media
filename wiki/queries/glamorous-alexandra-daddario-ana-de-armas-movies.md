# Glamorous, entertaining movies with Alexandra Daddario or Ana de Armas

The complete screened set is in [[wiki/sources/alexandra-daddario-ana-de-armas-recommendations]].

For the best balance of glamour, fun, and a sensible script, start with *Knives Out*. For Bond glamour, choose *No Time to Die*. For fast, uncomplicated action, choose *The Gray Man*. Alexandra Daddario's choices are more mood-dependent: *San Andreas* is popcorn spectacle, *We Have Always Lived in the Castle* is the more intelligent gothic option, and *The Layover* is knowingly silly time pass.
