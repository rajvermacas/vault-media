# Alexandra Daddario movie identification: two women competing for one man

The film is [[wiki/sources/alexandra-daddario-the-layover|*The Layover*]] (2017). Alexandra Daddario and Kate Upton play best friends who compete for the same man after their flight is rerouted and extended in St. Louis.

It fits a glamorous, silly time-pass mood, but the critical and audience ratings are very low. In India, the checked JustWatch snapshot listed it for rental on Amazon Video rather than as a free stream.
